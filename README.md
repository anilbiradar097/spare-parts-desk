# The Spare Parts Desk

**The Spare Parts Desk** is an intelligent inventory and procurement management solution designed to help spare parts businesses manage stock levels, identify inventory shortages, and make cost-effective replenishment decisions.

### Key Features
- **Inventory Management:** Track spare parts, available stock, and inventory requirements.
- **Stockout Detection:** Identify items that are running low or out of stock.
- **Smart Procurement:** Support purchasing decisions based on inventory needs and costs.
- **Stock Transfer Recommendations:** Help identify opportunities to transfer available stock between locations.
- **Cost Analysis:** Compare procurement and transfer options to support cost-effective decisions.
- **Decision Support Dashboard:** Present inventory insights and recommendations through a user-friendly interface.

### Objective
To improve inventory visibility, reduce unnecessary procurement expenses, prevent stock shortages, and support data-driven decision-making in spare parts operations.
